Cloud Nine: ADHD Planner — privacy policy

Updated November 23, 2026. Applies to the iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch app, its widgets and its share extension.

The short version
  • No account, no sign-in, and no analytics of our own.
  • Your plan is stored on your device. iCloud sync is optional and uses your private iCloud account, which only you can read.
  • The optional AI runs on your device. Your words are never sent to a server by Cloud Nine.
  • Calendars are read-only.
  • The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob, with personalized ads turned off. Google receives device and ad data to deliver and measure ads; it never receives your tasks, calendar, profile answers or check-ins.
  • Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple.

Data on this device

Your tasks, lists, routines, saved sessions and preferences are stored on this device. The app also saves any profile answers, check-ins or feedback you choose to record. You can use Cloud Nine without an account. If you turn on iCloud sync, your tasks, routines, lists, saved thoughts, leave-by plans, check-ins, progress history, profile answers and settings sync through your private iCloud account, which only you can read. A running timer and checkpoint notes stay on the device you’re using. You can choose to show your plan and timer on a paired Apple Watch.

Banner ads (free version)

The free version of Cloud Nine shows banner ads from Google AdMob, with personalized ads turned off. Cloud Nine never sends your tasks, calendar, profile answers or check-ins to AdMob. Google does receive device and network information (including an approximate location from your IP address), diagnostics and ad activity so it can deliver ads and measure them. In the European Union and the United Kingdom, Google’s consent form asks for your choices before ads load, and you can change them later under Settings → About this ad → Ad privacy choices. Removing ads with a purchase stops ad requests. Google’s own practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Help with typed plans

If enabled, Apple Intelligence or the optional Offline helper can interpret a planning request on this device. You review the proposed changes before saving. The app does not use profile answers such as age or diagnosis to interpret these requests.

The optional Offline helper

On iPhones without Apple Intelligence you can download the Offline helper, an on-device language model (Qwen3.5 2B). It downloads from Apple’s App Store servers like the app itself, runs entirely on your device and never sends your words anywhere. You can remove it in Settings → Help with typed plans.

What gets saved

A note you enter in Reset stays in that review. The app saves the steps you choose when you save a plan. Mood and energy check-ins are saved when you record them.

Calendar, notifications and your screen

Apple Calendar access is optional. The app reads events on this device so your plan can leave room for them; it never changes or adds events. Notifications follow your device’s notification and Focus settings. Widgets and Live Activities can show task names and session details on your screen. You can hide those details in Settings.

Google Calendar, only when you connect

If you connect directly to Google Calendar, Google handles sign-in and keeps track of the access you allow. Google may process account, device and sign-in usage information under its privacy policy. The app reads your calendar list and events from the calendars you show, with read-only permission. The app keeps loaded events only while it’s running. If you save a travel plan, its name and arrival time are saved in your local plan and can be included in a backup. Disconnect Google Calendar in Settings to remove the connection and clear loaded events.

Travel estimates

If you ask Apple Maps for an estimate, Apple receives the places you search for and the route details needed to estimate the trip. Cloud Nine saves the travel time you choose. For a calendar appointment, it also keeps a local link to the event and a fingerprint of its location so it can detect changes. The app does not save the addresses you enter for the estimate.

Apple Reminders

After you allow access, the app shows unfinished reminders from a list you choose. When you save a reminder, the app copies its title, due date, list name and a reference to the original. These details can appear in exports and device backups. Your original reminders stay unchanged.

Speaking tasks

The app uses the microphone when you choose to record. Your device turns speech into text. Cloud Nine keeps no audio recordings. You can review the transcript while drafting, and the app saves the tasks you choose. You can type your tasks if speech is unavailable.

Siri and Shortcuts

Apple handles these requests under your Siri settings. Unlock this device for Siri to read what’s next. Turn on “Hide task and routine names” to keep your next step in the app.

Reading a photo

You choose the photo or screenshot. Your device reads the text and adds it to your draft. Cloud Nine does not keep the image and saves only the tasks you choose to keep.

Saving text or a web link from another app

Only the text or link you choose to save is added to Saved thoughts. It stays on this device unless you include it in a backup. If you make a task from it and turn on iCloud sync, that task’s name can sync like other task names.

Exports and backups

An export can include task names, profile answers, check-ins, saved support conversations and feedback. You choose where to save or share it. Your device’s backup settings determine whether app data is included there. When you restore a backup, the app shows you what’s in the file first and keeps your previous save in case you need it.

Deleting data

“Clear app data” in Settings deletes your plan, setup draft and exports prepared on this device. It does not remove copies already synced to iCloud, shared copies or backups managed outside the app; delete those from iCloud settings or the backup they live in. Deleting the app removes its data from the device.

Purchases

Removing banner ads is an optional purchase (monthly or one-time) processed by Apple through the App Store. Cloud Nine receives only an anonymous confirmation that the purchase is active. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings.

Children

Cloud Nine is for adults and is not directed at children under 18. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

No analytics, no tracking

Cloud Nine does not run its own analytics service, has no server of its own, and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Feedback stays on your device until you choose to share it. Opening an external link takes you to that website and its privacy practices. Apple’s App Store, TestFlight and device backup services follow Apple’s own policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and material changes are described in the app’s release notes.
