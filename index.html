Common questions

An alert didn’t arrive.
Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Cloud Nine (Allow Notifications on), and that a Focus mode isn’t silencing the app. In Cloud Nine, Settings → Gentle reminders shows which alerts are on; a focus session also offers “Turn on timer alerts” if they’re off.
How do I make a day smaller?
Open the Reset tab and choose “Make a smaller plan”. Tell it the time and energy you have; it keeps fixed appointments and a short break, and nothing changes until you approve it. You can undo it afterwards.
What does “Try a smaller step” do?
It starts a two-minute version of the task (or five minutes, if you chose that in Settings → Review setup) and returns you to exactly where you paused.
How far ahead is the heads-up before a fixed time?
Five minutes by default. Change it to 10 or 15 under Settings → Gentle reminders → Before fixed times → How far ahead.
Does Cloud Nine change my calendar?
No. Apple and Google calendars are read-only. Cloud Nine only reads events so your plan can leave room for them.
How do I sync between my iPhone and iPad?
Turn on iCloud Sync under Settings → Your data on both devices. Tasks, routines, lists, saved thoughts and settings sync through your private iCloud account. A running timer stays on the device it started on.
I bought ad removal but still see ads.
Open Settings → Support Cloud Nine → Remove banner ads → Restore purchases. Subscriptions are managed in your Apple account settings.
How do I delete my data?
Settings → Your data → Clear app data removes everything on that device. Deleting the app does too. iCloud copies are removed from your iCloud settings. See the privacy policy.
Is this medical advice?
No. Cloud Nine supports everyday planning. It isn’t a diagnosis, treatment or crisis service. If you’re in crisis, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line.
